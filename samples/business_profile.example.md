# Lumen Nail Studio (fictional demo business)

- What we do: manicures, pedicures, gel and acrylic nails, nail art, brow shaping.
- Where: Unit 4, Harbour Walk (fictional address), ground floor next to the pharmacy. Free parking behind the building.
- Opening hours: Saturday to Thursday 10:00 to 21:00, Friday 14:00 to 21:00.
- Bookings: through the booking link in our Instagram bio, or by message. A team member confirms every booking personally.
- Walk-ins: welcome when a chair is free, but booking is recommended at weekends.
- Products: vegan, cruelty-free polish brands. Patch tests available on request.
- Gift cards: available in store.
- Prices: on the menu in store and on the booking page. Staff confirm prices personally.
- Languages: English and Arabic.
